Data-Driven Customer Retention Playbook

1. Transition Month-to-Month to Annual Plans

Month-to-Month accounts churn at over 53%, compared to under 12% for Two-Year plans. Introduce a 15% annual prepaid discount to lock in commitment and extend customer lifetime.

2. 90-Day Early Onboarding Intervention

Over 48% of cancellations happen within the first 6 months. Deploy automated customer success touchpoints at Day 14, 45, and 75 to guarantee product adoption.

3. Bundle Free Tech Support & Security

Accounts with Tech Support enabled exhibit 60% lower churn. Bundle complimentary 6-month premium support with all Fiber optic and higher-tier subscriptions.

4. Proactive Ticket Resolution Triggers

Accounts logging 3+ support tickets churn at double the average rate. Establish an automated escalation protocol for senior engineering support whenever a customer logs >2 tickets.
